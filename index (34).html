Got Questions?

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Can absolute beginners with no prior dance experience join?

Yes! More than 60% of our new students start with zero dance background. Our Starter courses break down basic counts, posture, footwork, and muscle memory at an encouraging, comfortable pace.

What is the minimum age requirement for kids?

Our Little Champs program welcomes children from 4 years and above. Classes emphasize playful rhythm coordination, posture, and joy before progressing into technical choreography.

Do you offer wedding and couple choreography?

Yes, our Wedding Choreography wing crafts customized entry dances, couple performances, flashmobs, and family sangeet sets with home or studio rehearsals.

Can I book a free trial class before committing?

Absolutely. We invite every prospective student to experience one complimentary studio trial class. Call or WhatsApp +91 8935964374 to reserve your trial slot.
