Where Rhythm Meets Excellence

Located at the heart of Lalbagh, Patna, Spectrum Dance Academy (स्पेक्ट्रम डांस एकेडमी) stands as Bihar's premier dance training institution. We provide comprehensive artistic guidance for toddlers, youth, working professionals, and wedding families. Our studio operates 24 Hours with sound-engineered acoustic floors, mirrored rehearsal suites, and certified choreographers.

Our Mission

To nurture artistic confidence, rhythm, and stage mastery across all age groups through disciplined, joyful, and internationally aligned choreography training.

Our Vision

To be the pinnacle performing arts sanctuary in East India, empowering students to compete on national television stages and global championships.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Can absolute beginners join Spectrum Dance Academy?

Yes, absolutely! Over 60% of our students start with zero dance training. Our Beginner classes break down counts, body postures, footwork, and rhythm at an encouraging, friendly pace.

Is the academy really Open 24 Hours?

Yes! We offer 24/7 flexible studio access. We conduct regular morning, evening, and late batches, alongside round-the-clock booking for wedding choreographies, solo practice, and video shoots.

Where exactly is the studio located in Patna?

We are conveniently located at: 1st Floor, Sumitra Market, Opposite Bank of Baroda, Beside Gopal Market, Naya Tola, Lalbagh, Patna, Bihar – 800004.

Do you choreograph Sangeet and wedding events?

Yes, our Wedding Choreography package is one of Patna's most celebrated services. We design custom couple dances, bride-groom entries, flashmobs, and family routines with studio or home sessions.

Can I book a free trial session?

Yes! You can reserve one complimentary demo class. Simply tap on "Claim Free Trial Slot" below, call +91 8935964374, or message us on WhatsApp.
